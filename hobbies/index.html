💻 Tech & IoT

🌱 Sow by Season

An Australian garden planner: what to plant, when to plant it and how much — with your local climate, weather and seasons. Your garden stays on your own device.

Try it in your browser Download for Android

No download needed in the browser. On iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Android app adds reminders, a home-screen widget and automatic backup.

Geranium Pre-release

Personal finance for Australian households: see where your money went, what it really costs to live and what’s coming up — with budgets, forecasts and an Australian tax estimate. Your data stays encrypted on your own device, and it never connects to your bank.

Try it in your browser Download for your computer

Geranium is still a pre-release, so expect some rough edges and keep a backup of your data. No account and nothing uploaded. Choose Explore with demo data to look around first. The desktop app is for Windows, Mac and Linux. Not financial advice.
