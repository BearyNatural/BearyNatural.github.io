      Pencil each number on the piece as you cut it. The number on a piece's left edge is its height as drawn, and the number along its bottom is its width. Dashed outlines are pieces cut out of a wall: the hatch door and the storage flap. This step's pieces are highlighted. Tap a piece to jump to the step where it goes on.

      Sizes are for your measured bare frame: 1803 long × 860 wide × 913 high, outside of the steel, with the clad half 895 from the hatch end to the middle of the middle post. There's ply for the floor or the top deck on the run half, not both.
